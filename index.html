Talogy Marketing – Q1 Highlights & Q2 Outlook

Rebuilding the Engine and Establishing Leading Indicators

Q1 2026 Update Prepared for ELT

Q1 Summary: Rebuilding the Marketing Engine

The first quarter focused on rebuilding marketing infrastructure and team capacity required to support Talogy's growth targets. With the Americas team rebuilding over a 14–16 week period, meaningful campaign activity began in March.

Q1 priority: establish a foundation for scalable, cross-regional pipeline generation.

Key Milestones Achieved

1) Campaign Engine Rebuilt and Activated

  • First new campaign launched: March 4 – Caliper for Sales
  • Generated $70K pipeline, including a pending Panasonic deal
  • Campaign framework is now operational and repeatable

2) Scalable Campaign Infrastructure Established

  • New content can now be introduced monthly
  • Programs can expand from enterprise into midmarket
  • Digital paid media integration now enabled via Platty

Outcome: continuous in-market campaigns, not isolated launches.

3) Website Conversion Optimization Program Started

  • New Contact Us journey and homepage redesign
  • Book a Demo via Calendly + product CTA improvements
  • Sample report previews and AI chatbot enhancements
  • Form optimization testing now underway

Why MQL YoY Comparisons Are Misleading

  1. Strategy shift to quality: fewer, higher-converting leads over raw volume.
  2. Structural changes: reduced headcount and budget in Europe; constrained capacity in APAC/MEA; AMER rebuilding.
  3. Initiative mix evolving: thought leadership, premium events, strategic campaigns, PR, and brand work influence pipeline later in cycle.

Primary performance lens

Conversion Rate + Pipeline Growth

These now provide a clearer picture of effectiveness than MQL count alone.

Operational Challenges Impacting Performance

SDR Performance (Americas)

  • Outbound View team underperforming
  • Performance review in progress
  • Go / No-Go decision by month-end
  • Alternatives under review: new vendor or in-house hire

Website Conversion Gaps

  • Traffic growth outpacing conversion growth
  • Heatmap UX testing active
  • Conversion expert consultations active
  • Site redesign and CTA improvements underway

Regional Capacity Constraints

  • Europe: 1/3 team loss across 9 countries, 6 languages
  • APAC/MEA: lean teams across multiple markets and partners
  • AMER: shared HC may limit scale; full-time investment to evaluate in H2

What Will Drive Success in Q2 and Beyond

  1. Continuous campaign engine: monthly launches in AMER with APAC expansion potential.
  2. Paid media acceleration: Platty amplification to extend reach and lead generation.
  3. Website conversion optimization: convert existing traffic into more qualified leads and demos.
  4. Improved sales alignment: tighter MQL-to-SQL and pipeline focus for revenue impact.

Strategic perspective: Q1 was about rebuilding the marketing engine. Q2 is about scaling it.

  • Campaigns now in market
  • Events in full swing
  • Pipeline generation beginning
  • Infrastructure in place for continuous execution
